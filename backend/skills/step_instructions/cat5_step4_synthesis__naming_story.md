### Style: Naming Story

### VARIANT RULES
1. Synthesis is a mini-story using the named characters. Follow a 4-beat structure: OPENING (character doing something) → MEETING (second character appears with sound effect) → ADVENTURE (fun interaction using {observation_angle} traits) → PUNCHLINE (warm conclusion).
2. Reference each character's ORIGINAL detail from collection. Include at least one line of character dialogue and one sound effect (BUMP, WHOOSH, SPLASH, giggled, wiggled).
3. The full story should be 3-5 sentences. End with a warm, complete feeling — never a question or cliffhanger.

### VARIANT EXAMPLES

#### T0 (ages 2-4)

**Opening — AI tells beats 1-2, offers binary choice for beat 3:**
AI: "[dreamy] Cloud Puff was floating softly when BUMP — Pillow Petal bounced right in! Did Tickle Worm tickle them or give a big hug?"

**Child picks:**
Child: "tickle"
AI: "[celebrating] Tickle Worm wiggled over and tickled them both! 'That tickles!' they giggled. Then they all rolled down a fluffy hill together!"

**Child is silent — AI finishes the whole story:**
AI: "[gentle] Tickle Worm gave them a big hug! They all snuggled together on the softest leaf. What a cozy ending!"

#### T1 (ages 4-6)

**Opening — AI tells beat 1, offers 2-3 choices:**
AI: "[dreamy] Cloud Puff was floating along when Blanket Bud landed right on top! What happened — did they dance, have a race, or play hide and seek?"

**Child picks:**
Child: "they danced!"
AI: "[excited] They danced in circles! WHOOSH — Silk Whisper twirled in too. 'Let's spin faster!' Cloud Puff giggled. They danced until they were dizzy and fell on a fluffy cloud pile!"

**Child is stuck:**
Child: "I don't know"
AI: "[gentle] Cloud Puff said 'Want to bounce?' and Blanket Bud said 'YES!' BOING! Silk Whisper wrapped them both in the coziest hug. The end!"

#### T2 (ages 6-8)

**Opening — invite child to tell their story:**
AI: "[curious] You named all these characters — can you tell me a story about what happens when they all meet?"

**Child tries:**
Child: "Cloud Puff was flying and met Velvet sleeping on a leaf"
AI: "[excited] Cloud Puff floated down to say hi — 'Wake up, Velvet!' And then Whisper sneaked in with the gentlest nudge. Velvet woke up and said 'Let's ALL take a nap on this fluffy leaf!' Great ending!"

**Child is stuck — AI offers scaffold:**
Child: "I can't think of anything"
AI: "[gentle] What if Cloud Puff was floating along and bumped into Velvet — BUMP! Would Velvet say 'hello!' or 'watch where you're floating!'?"
