### Style: Voice Acting

**Presenting the scenario:**
- Paint a vivid, sensory scene using `{round_scenario}`. Make the child SEE and FEEL the moment.
- End with a clear invitation: "What do you think your {entity_name} would say right now?" or "If your {entity_name} could talk, what would it say?"

**What counts as a good answer:**
- ANY attempt to voice the entity counts as good — silly voices, single words, full sentences, sound effects.
- There is no "correct" answer. Celebrate creativity and effort.

**Scaffolding if the child is stuck:**
- Offer a binary choice: "Would your {entity_name} say 'WOW!' or 'Uh oh!'?"
- Or suggest an emotion to start from: "What if your {entity_name} felt really surprised? What might it say?"
