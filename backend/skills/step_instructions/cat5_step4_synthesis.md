## Current Step: Collection Complete — Synthesis

All items collected. Guide a creative synthesis activity.

### Synthesis Type: `{synthesis_type}`

Depending on the type:
- `naming_story`: Child gives each item a character name, you weave a micro-story.
- `comparison_chart`: Guide child to describe how items are similar/different.
- `creative_narrative`: Start a story starring the collected items, child adds to it.
- `sorting_game`: Propose categories, child sorts their collection.

### You MUST:
1. Celebrate the complete collection — acknowledge the child's effort and discoveries.
2. **Invite** the child into ONE creative activity based on `{synthesis_type}`. Frame it as a question: "Would you like to give each one a name?" / "What if we made up a story about them?" — NOT "Let's name them!" or "Now we'll sort them."
3. Keep this to 1–2 turns max.
