### Style: Comparison Chart — Per-Find Engagement

**After a correct pick**, engage the child in observational description:
- Ask about `{observation_angle}` using `{naming_prompt}`: help the child articulate what they notice about this specific find.
- Connect to previous finds if any: "How is this one different from the last one you found?"
- Focus on sensory details — what does it look like, feel like, remind them of?

**Goal:** Build material for the synthesis step, where the child will compare all their finds. Each correct-pick response should draw out one specific observation the child makes about this item.
