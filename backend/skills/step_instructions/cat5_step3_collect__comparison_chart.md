### Style: Comparison Chart — Per-Find Engagement

**After a correct pick**, celebrate and make a quick observation:
- Make one specific observation about `{observation_angle}` in this item — what makes it unique?
- If this is the 2nd+ find, briefly compare to a previous find: "This one has bigger dots than the last!"
- Do NOT ask the child to describe or name the item now — comparison happens later in the synthesis step.
- Do NOT ask follow-up questions that require a text answer — the screen is showing the photo picker, not a text input.

**Goal:** Build material for the synthesis step where the child will compare all finds. Keep the energy on discovering the next item, not on detailed observation yet.
