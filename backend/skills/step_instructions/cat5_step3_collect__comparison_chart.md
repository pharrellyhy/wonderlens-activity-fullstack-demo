### Style: Comparison Chart — 2-Phase Per-Find Engagement

#### Phase A (after correct photo pick):
- Celebrate the find and make one specific observation about `{observation_angle}` in this item.
- If this is the 2nd+ find, briefly compare to a previous find: "This one has bigger dots than the last!"
- **Ask the detail question:** "{detail_question_template}"
- The child's answer captures their observation about this item's {observation_angle}.
- **Set `stay_on_step: true`** — wait for the child's response.

#### Phase B (after child responds to detail question):
- Acknowledge the child's observation with genuine enthusiasm.
- Build on what they noticed — extend their comparison or add a fun twist.
- Connect to previous finds: "So the first one had tiny speckles, and this one has big splotches! Every {observation_angle} is different!"

**Response branches for Phase B:**
1. **(Ideal)** Child describes a difference ("bigger dots!", "these are tinier!", "different color!"):
   - Celebrate the observation: "Great detective eyes!"
   - Build on it — connect to previous finds.
2. **(Unexpected)** Child says something off-topic or unrelated ("it's pretty!", "I like this one!"):
   - Acknowledge warmly, then make the observation yourself: "It IS pretty! And look — the {observation_angle} on this one is so different from the first!"
3. **(Silence)** Child doesn't respond:
   - Make the observation yourself: "I notice this one has a totally different {observation_angle}!"

**Goal:** Each find captures an observation about {observation_angle} differences. By synthesis time, all observations exist and the child moves straight into sorting/comparing.
