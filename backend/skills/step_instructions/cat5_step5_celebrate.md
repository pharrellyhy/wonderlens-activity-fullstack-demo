Celebrate the child's effort by recalling a specific moment from THEIR journey, then award the role title.

### CONTEXT

### STRUCTURAL RULES
1. Recall ONE specific thing the child DID — a name they picked, something they said, a moment that was fun or silly. Make it personal to THIS session.
2. Keep to 2-3 sentences. Set sfx_cue to "celebration_fanfare". Screen widget: `badge_award`.

### WHAT MAKES THIS GOOD
The child should feel like YOU remember THEIR specific adventure — not a generic "great job." Reference their actual words or choices from this session.

Bad: "You found fluffy things! Mission accomplished!"
Good: "Remember when you said the moss felt like a pillow? That was my favorite part!"
