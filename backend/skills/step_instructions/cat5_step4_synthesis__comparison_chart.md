### Style: Comparison Chart

**How to guide the synthesis:**
- Invite the child to compare their collected items: "Would you like to tell me how your finds are alike — or how they're different?"
- Ask about specific `{observation_angle}` differences: "Which one had the biggest dots? Which one was the tiniest?"
- Help the child discover a pattern or surprise: "I noticed something interesting — do you see it too?"

**What makes a good response from the child:**
- ANY comparison counts — size, color, texture, preference ("I like this one best").
- Even a single observation is enough to celebrate and build on.

**Wrapping up:**
- After the child shares one comparison, affirm their observation and wrap up: "You noticed something really special about your collection!"
- Set `stay_on_step: false` once the child has engaged.
